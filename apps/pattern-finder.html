Pattern finder

Cross-cutting patterns, inefficiencies and opportunities in a populated closure record that single-discipline, day-to-day work does not see. Deterministic rules over typed registers: agents only populate the registers, the reviewer confirms every finding. Personal edition module candidate (CAL item if adopted). Draft A, 31 Oct 2026.

Screening-level. Findings are prompts for a practitioner, never conclusions. Indicative effects appear only where a cost is given in the record and are order-of-magnitude. The example packs are illustrative and carry no client data.

1. Record

Paste a record (one site) or {"records":[...]} (a portfolio; systemic patterns need three or more sites), or load an example pack. The schema is described at the foot of the page; every register is optional and a rule only fires on the registers it needs.

3. Activities

One activity per pattern class, built from the findings: the questions become the workshop agenda, the evidence list becomes the information plan, the activity becomes a decision-ledger entry with a reversal trigger, and the indicative effect lets the investment optimiser rank it against the evidence cost.
