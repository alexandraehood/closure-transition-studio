# Agent instruction amendments - CAL-10 rating rule and the deterministic engine (Draft A, 30 Oct 2026)

Applies to: the Strategist agent (Workbook Output Mode, Tables A/B/C), Technical & Evidence Review, Regulatory Obligations, Stakeholder & Social, Cost & Liability Optimization, Screening Framework & Scoring (tests), Portfolio Synthesis, Deliverable Assembly & QA (checks). Paste each block verbatim into the named section; remove the sentences it replaces.

## Where the "undocumented = 2" bias lives today
1. No drafting agent carries a rating scale with anchors. The specialists return Draft findings with a three-level confidence (High / Medium / Low) and E1-E4 grades; the Strategist's workbook tables then take a 1-5 rating. With no anchor for "not evidenced", the rating defaults to the floor of the third band (2 = 40 points) whenever the document is missing. Sixty-nine of the 104 desktop ratings on the four pilot records are a 2; the pilot site A site team confirmed 3 or 4 on 16 of the 18 criteria the desk had at 2 or lower.
2. The Information Confidence Gate ("If Low, all subsequent criteria are interpreted qualified ... do not average Low into a higher composite") pushes the rating down as well as the confidence - the same uncertainty is charged twice, once in the rating and once in the confidence.
3. The Common Evidence Taxonomy grades any company document older than five years E3 regardless of whether the decision needs newer data (fixed by CAL-19), and "confidence never exceeds the weakest mandatory provenance field" caps the rating's confidence by paperwork, not by the condition of the asset.
4. Nothing requires the agent to state what would move the rating, so the room has no deliberation range to start from (CAL-29).

## Block A - RATING RULE (CAL-10): rate the condition, grade the evidence - separately
Insert in every specialist agent under COMMON CONFIDENCE MODEL, and in the Strategist under WORKBOOK OUTPUT MODE before the table definitions.

RATING RULE (framework calibration item CAL-10, in force from v3.4)
- A rating is the best estimate of the CONDITION of the asset on the balance of the evidence available, on the 1-5 anchor scale for that criterion. It is never a statement about how much paper exists.
- The evidence GRADE (E1 primary legal/permit/design record or independent report; E2 regulator correspondence, audited data, ITRB/EoR report; E3 company or consultant document not independently reviewed; E4 inference, anecdote or unverifiable; W a minuted statement of the site team) is recorded beside the rating and carries how sure we are. "Undocumented" is a GRADE (E4), not a rating. Never write 2 because a document is missing; write the best estimate and grade it E4.
- Caps that follow from the grade: an E4-based best estimate cannot exceed 3; a W-based best estimate cannot exceed 4; a facility rated on visual evidence only cannot exceed 3 and one with no assessment cannot exceed 2 (CAL-17).
- Every rating carries: (a) the rating used, (b) the grade, (c) the lower and upper bound by the grade rules (E1/E2 point; E3 up to +1 capped at 4; E4 -1/+1 capped at 3; W -1/+0), (d) the two values when the evidence supports two readings (deliberation range, CAL-29), (e) the citation - Evidence ID, document title, page or clause - or the words "no document; basis: <statement or inference>", (f) one sentence on what would move the rating one level up and one level down.
- Data currency is decision-relevant (CAL-19): a document is "current" if the decision the rating supports does not need newer data; it is not downgraded for age alone.
- Facility-split criteria (T1 water, T3 geotechnical, T6 closure performance): give one sub-rating per facility (TSF, heap leach, pits/underground, waste rock and landforms, water system); the criterion rating is the weakest APPLICABLE facility (CAL-17). Facilities that do not exist at the site are "not applicable", not low.
- Low confidence never lowers a rating and never blocks Tier 2 thinking (CAL-16). It changes what the result may support (confidence class, Block C) and what goes on the information request list.
- The agent never computes a dimension score, a Base Readiness Score, a TRI, a category or a confidence class. Those are produced by the deterministic engine (Block B). An agent output that contains a TRI or a category it computed itself fails QA.

## Block B - DETERMINISTIC CALCULATION (CAL-39): the engine of record
Insert in the Strategist under WORKBOOK OUTPUT MODE and in Screening Framework & Scoring under FRAMEWORK STRUCTURE TESTS; reference from the Deliverable Assembly & QA DETERMINISTIC INPUT GATE.

- One engine, versioned (v3.2-PILOT as built; v3.3 candidate; v3.4 candidate), implemented identically in the site-record workbook formulas and in the LATAF engine code (lataf_engine.py). The code is the test oracle; the workbook is the record of decision. A rule change is a CAL item with a version string, a regression run on the four pilot records and the reference example, and a new engine version - never an edit to a prompt.
- Inputs: 26 criterion records (rating used, grade, bounds, deliberation range, fatal flag, facility sub-ratings), jurisdiction sub-indices, pathway and its institution dependence, Tier 2 counts after de-duplication (critical assumptions not already carried by a barrier or gap; open barriers; unresolved monitoring deficiencies; dominant risks with known-ineffective controls), site-set parameters (currency and price year, currency benchmark, contact-water definition).
- Calculation (v3.3/v3.4): dimension score = 20 x sum(weight within dimension x rating used); BRS = mean of the five dimension scores (0.2 each); TRI = BRS x JCA + TOA share x BRS, where JCA is 0.85 / 0.90 / 0.95 / 1.00 for JCI 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 (applied when the pathway is institution-dependent under v3.3; always under the v3.4 option, CAL-27 pending) and the TOA share is the de-duplicated Tier 2 deduction (negative) with its cap; the CAF multiplier is off (CAL-12). The engine also runs at the lower and upper bound of every criterion to give TRI_low and TRI_high.
- Result: category by threshold set (A 80/60/40 as built; B 70/50/30; C 75/55/35 - CAL-14); confidence class Measured / Indicated / Inferred from the weighted share of criteria evidenced E1-E2 (>= 0.60 and weakest evidence sub-rating >= 4 = Measured; < 0.30 or weakest sub-rating <= 2 = Inferred; else Indicated); only Measured may be reported Transition ready. Decision category (headline, CAL-37): a fatal flag or TRI_high below the barrier threshold = Escalate decision; bounds in different categories = Information-limited, Proceed with validation; Significant barriers at both bounds = Reassess pathway; Achievable or Ready at both bounds = Proceed.
- Reference values the engine must reproduce before any run is accepted: pilot site A v3.2 record 23.868; v3.3 desktop 37.128 [37.128-43.512]; v3.3 site-confirmed 57.288 [57.288-61.152]; v3.4 site-confirmed 48.544 [44.696-51.652]; pilot site B 25.872 / 36.036; pilot site C 32.86 / 45.05; pilot site D 26.352 / 39.312. The open edition ships only the anonymous Reference vector A (57.288 / 36.828 / 55.242); the other chains stayed with the client.

## Block C - PROBABILISTIC READINESS AND VALUE OF INFORMATION (CAL-40)
Insert in Screening Framework & Scoring (CALIBRATION RUNS) and Portfolio Synthesis (RECOGNIZED COMPARATIVE METRIC FAMILY).
- The engine draws every criterion from a triangular distribution over its bounds (mode = rating used), and the structural parameters from their admissible sets (TOA share from the current deduction to zero as Tier 2 items close; JCA 0.90 or 1.00 under CAL-27; threshold set A/B/C), 20,000 draws, and reports P10 / P50 / P90 TRI and the probability of each category. "Information-limited" is reported as those probabilities.
- Value of information: for every open gap, the change in category probability if the gap were closed (criterion fixed at its upper bound, or the Tier 2 item removed), divided by the cost of closing it = priority on the information plan. The list is the priced shopping list the site takes to its budget.
- Pilot site A, site-confirmation workshop: rating uncertainty alone leaves the category unchanged (v3.4 P10-P90 47.5-49.2, Significant barriers 100%); adding the structural uncertainty (TOA -16% to 0, JCA 0.90/1.00) gives 50.4-63.3 with Achievable 26% under set A and 92% under set B. The information that matters at pilot site A is its Tier 2 items (two open assumptions, one barrier, three monitoring items and one risk) and the rule decisions, not more rating precision.

## Block D - QA checks to add (Deliverable Assembly & QA, section 3.4)
- Every rating row has a grade, bounds, a citation or a "no document; basis" statement, and a one-level-up / one-level-down sentence; a rating of 2 with grade E4 and no basis is a defect.
- No TRI, dimension score, category or confidence class appears in an agent output unless it was produced by the engine with its version string and reference-value check.
- Facility-split criteria carry a sub-rating per applicable facility.
- Currency and price year appear beside every cost figure; no conversion unless the rate and date are stated.
